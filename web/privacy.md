# Privacy

This is a personal blog. {{author}} runs it and is responsible for the personal data described here (the
controller under the GDPR). Questions about this page can be sent through the [contact page](contact.html).

## What This Site Collects
This site sets no cookies of its own and collects nothing itself. The images and styles are served from this site itself. Google Analytics runs only if you allow it,
and the contact form and its spam check load only on the [contact page](contact.html); both are described below, as is the hosting.

## Google Analytics
If you allow it in the consent bar, this site loads Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are read. Google
Analytics then sets cookies whose names start with `_ga`. What Google collects is described in the
[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).

Your choice, allow or decline, is kept in your browser's local storage so the bar does not ask again. You can withdraw
consent at any time with the "Analytics choice" button in the footer: it deletes the analytics cookies and asks again.

## Hosting
The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you open a page, GitHub, Inc. handles your request under the
[GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
The legal basis is a legitimate interest in running a secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

## Contact Form
If you send a message from the [contact page](contact.html), your name, email address and message go to
Web3Forms, under the [Web3Forms Privacy Policy](https://web3forms.com/privacy). I use them only to reply. The legal basis is a legitimate
interest in answering you (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

To keep out spam, the contact page loads hCaptcha, which checks that a person is sending the message. Its use is described in the
[hCaptcha Privacy Policy](https://www.hcaptcha.com/privacy). The legal basis is a legitimate interest in protecting the
form from abuse (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

## Your Rights
This site has no database and keeps no visitor records. For the data held by [Google](https://policies.google.com/privacy),
[GitHub](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement), [Web3Forms](https://web3forms.com/privacy) and
[hCaptcha](https://www.hcaptcha.com/privacy), use the controls and contact details in their privacy policies. You can withdraw your analytics consent at any time with the
"Analytics choice" button in the footer; that does not affect what was done before. If you live in the EU or the EEA, you can also complain to the data
protection authority in your country.

Data may be transferred to and processed in the United States. Where required by law, the providers above use
appropriate safeguards for international transfers.
