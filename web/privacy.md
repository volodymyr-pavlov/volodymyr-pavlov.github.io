# Privacy

This is a personal blog. {{author}} runs it and is responsible for the personal data described here (the
controller under the GDPR). Questions about this page go to [email](mailto:{{email}}).

## What This Site Collects
Nothing of its own. There are no cookies, no analytics, no forms, and no content embedded from other sites. Fonts and
images are served from this site itself.

## Hosting
The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you open a page, GitHub, Inc. receives your IP address and browser details and
may keep them in server logs to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. GitHub is based in the United States and
processes this data under the
[GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
The legal basis is a legitimate interest in running a secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

## Email
If you write to me, I use your address and message only to reply, and delete them once the conversation is over unless
you ask me to keep them. The legal basis is the same legitimate interest in answering you.
